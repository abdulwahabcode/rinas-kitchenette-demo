Our Story

Serving Lahore Since 1997

Ziafat began as a six-table eatery with one coal grill and a family recipe book. Twenty-eight years later, the grill is bigger, the hall is grander — but the recipes haven't changed a word.

Every seekh kabab is still hand-minced each morning. Every karahi still starts with desi ghee. That's why three generations of Lahoris celebrate their daawats here.

  • Real charcoal, always — no gas shortcuts on the BBQ
  • Halal & fresh daily — meat sourced every morning
  • Family hall + private daawat rooms for events
