Our story

A home bakery,
grown up gracefully.

Rina’s Kitchenette started as one woman’s home kitchen and a stubborn belief: that a cake should taste like it was made for someone you love. Years later, that belief runs three branches across Pakistan — but every sponge is still mixed, every croissant still folded, and every order still packed like it’s for family.
